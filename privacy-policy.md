# Privacy Policy

## TL;DR

VO2max Lab is a local-first Android app. We do not run any backend server. We do not collect, transmit, share, or sell your personal data. Everything you record (heart rate samples, GPS tracks, treadmill speed, workout history, profile information) stays on your device. The only network traffic generated by the app is requests to OpenFreeMap to download the map tiles you see during outdoor workouts. Uninstalling the app deletes all data we ever held about you, because we never held any in the first place.

If you only read one section, this is it.

## 3. Data we process

VO2max Lab handles the following categories of data **on your device only**.

### 3.1 Profile data

You enter your weight, height, age, biological sex, optional resting heart rate, optional maximum heart rate, and a profile name. The app supports multiple profiles per device for households or coaches.

- **Storage:** Local Room database (SQLite) inside the app's private storage directory. Other apps cannot read it without root access.
- **Use:** Computing predicted V̇O2max (Cooper formula, Aspenes 2011 non-exercise estimator), Tanaka maximum heart rate, age-graded fitness comparisons.
- **Transmission off-device:** None.
- **Retention:** Until you delete the profile in-app or uninstall the app.

### 3.2 Heart rate

Heart rate samples come either from Health Connect (required permission `android.permission.health.READ_HEART_RATE`) or, on Android 13 and earlier, from the deprecated Body Sensors API (`android.permission.BODY_SENSORS`) when no Health Connect source is available.

- **Storage:** Local Room database, attached to each Cooper or 4x4 workout you complete.
- **Use:** Real-time HR display during workouts, average and peak HR computation in the post-workout summary, training-zone analysis.
- **Transmission off-device:** None.
- **Health Connect:** We only **read** heart rate. Health Connect remains the source of truth and you can revoke our access at any time from the Health Connect settings.

### 3.3 V̇O2max readings (read + write)

We declare two Health Connect permissions for V̇O2max: `READ_VO2_MAX` to display historical results and `WRITE_VO2_MAX` to write the result of each Cooper or 4x4 test back into Health Connect, **on your device**, so other health apps you use can see it.

- **Storage:** Health Connect (managed by Google's Health Connect app, on-device) plus our own Room database for the workout summary.
- **Transmission off-device:** None. Health Connect is an on-device data exchange platform; the data does not leave your phone unless **you** authorise a third-party app inside Health Connect to sync it.

### 3.4 Exercise sessions and distance (Health Connect read)

Permissions `READ_EXERCISE` and `READ_DISTANCE` let the app surface external workouts (e.g. an outdoor run logged by another fitness app) when you choose to associate them with a fitness-age recalculation. We never write exercise sessions ourselves.

- **Use:** Read-only display in the history screen.
- **Transmission off-device:** None.

### 3.5 GPS location

GPS is used **only** during two specific activities:

- **Cooper test, outdoor mode** (`Fase D WS4`): the FusedLocationProvider samples your position every ~3 seconds for 12 minutes to compute the distance covered.
- **Norwegian 4x4 test, outdoor mode** (`Fase H WS-H3`): the same provider samples during the four high-intensity intervals and three active recoveries (~43 minutes total).

Permissions involved: `ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION`, `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`, `FOREGROUND_SERVICE`, `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION`.

- **Background access:** **None.** Location sampling stops the moment the workout ends, you press Stop, or the foreground service is killed.
- **Geofencing or activity recognition:** **None.**
- **Storage:** The path is stored as a list of timestamped `(latitude, longitude, accuracy)` points in the local Room database, attached to that single workout record.
- **Transmission off-device:** None. We do not reverse-geocode locations, we do not look up nearby places, we do not ping any server about where you are.
- **Map tiles:** Rendering your path on a map requires fetching map tiles from OpenFreeMap (see Section 4). The tile request URL contains tile coordinates, not your exact GPS coordinates, but it does reveal the rough area you are looking at to OpenFreeMap's CDN.

### 3.6 Bluetooth (BT FTMS treadmill)

Permissions `BLUETOOTH_SCAN` (declared with `neverForLocation` flag), `BLUETOOTH_CONNECT`, and on Android 11 and earlier the legacy `BLUETOOTH` and `BLUETOOTH_ADMIN`.

- **Use:** Discover and connect to a tapis-roulant supporting the Fitness Machine Service (FTMS, standard service UUID `0x1826`). During a workout we read the treadmill's reported total distance and instantaneous speed and, only for the 4x4 test (`Fase M WS-M1`), we send target-speed write commands so the treadmill can auto-adjust between intervals and recoveries.
- **Storage:** The treadmill's reported speed log is stored locally in Room as part of the workout record. We do **not** store, log, or transmit the treadmill's MAC address or device identifier off the device.
- **Transmission off-device:** None.
- **`neverForLocation` flag:** Declared so Android does not infer location from BLE scans. We genuinely do not use BLE for location.

### 3.7 Foreground service notifications

Permission `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` is required from Android 13 (API 33) onward to display the persistent notification that keeps the workout's foreground service alive while your screen is off.

- **Use:** Show the running workout countdown so the OS does not kill the timing service mid-test.
- **Personal data in notifications:** Workout type and remaining time only.

### 3.8 Vibration

Permission `VIBRATE` provides haptic feedback at workout phase transitions (interval start, recovery start, test end). No data implication.

### 3.9 Network state

Permission `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` is read by the MapLibre map rendering library to detect Wi-Fi vs mobile data switches and adjust tile caching behaviour. It does not transmit anything.

## 4. Network traffic generated by the app

VO2max Lab makes outbound HTTPS requests **only** to:

- **`tiles.openfreemap.org`**: vector map tiles and the style descriptor (`style.json`) used by the MapLibre map view during Cooper outdoor mode and 4x4 outdoor mode. Requests go out only while a map is on screen during a tracked workout.

That is the entire list. There is no analytics endpoint, no crash-reporting endpoint, no advertising endpoint, no remote configuration endpoint, no authentication endpoint.

OpenFreeMap is operated by a third party. The information they receive in a tile request consists of: your IP address, the tile coordinates (z, x, y) and your User-Agent. We do not control OpenFreeMap. Their privacy posture is documented at [`https://openfreemap.org`](https://openfreemap.org).

If you never use the outdoor map (i.e. you only run the Cooper or 4x4 test in indoor mode or with a treadmill), the app makes **zero** network requests after install.

Of these, only Google Play Services Location and MapLibre have any network capability, and we configure them as described above (location: device-local; MapLibre: OpenFreeMap tiles only).

## 6. Permissions summary

Every permission declared in the Android manifest, in plain language:

| Permission | Why we ask for it | Optional? |
|---|---|---|
| `health.READ_HEART_RATE` | Read heart rate during workouts | Yes (granted via Health Connect) |
| `health.READ_VO2_MAX` | Show your historical V̇O2max from Health Connect | Yes |
| `health.WRITE_VO2_MAX` | Save Cooper / 4x4 result back to Health Connect, on-device | Yes |
| `health.READ_EXERCISE` | Display external workouts in history | Yes |
| `health.READ_DISTANCE` | Display external distance records in history | Yes |
| `BODY_SENSORS` | Fallback heart-rate read on Android 13 and earlier | Yes |
| `ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION` | GPS distance during outdoor Cooper / outdoor 4x4 | Yes (only needed if you use outdoor mode) |
| `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION` | GPS coarse fallback during outdoor mode | Yes |
| `BLUETOOTH_SCAN` (`neverForLocation`) | Discover FTMS treadmills | Yes (only for treadmill mode) |
| `BLUETOOTH_CONNECT` | Connect to FTMS treadmill | Yes |
| `BLUETOOTH` / `BLUETOOTH_ADMIN` (`maxSdkVersion=30`) | Same as above on Android 11 and earlier | Yes |
| `INTERNET` | Fetch OpenFreeMap tiles when the outdoor map is on screen | Yes (no map = no traffic) |
| `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` | MapLibre tile-cache behaviour | Yes |
| `FOREGROUND_SERVICE`, `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_HEALTH`, `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION`, `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_CONNECTED_DEVICE` | Keep workout timer alive while screen is off | No (Android requirement) |
| `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` | Show the foreground-service notification (Android 13+) | Yes (system grant) |
| `VIBRATE` | Haptic feedback on phase transitions | No (granted at install, no data implication) |

You can revoke any runtime permission at any time from Android Settings → Apps → VO2max Lab → Permissions, or, for Health Connect, inside the Health Connect app. The relevant feature will degrade gracefully (e.g. revoking GPS disables outdoor mode but the app continues to work).

## 7. Children's privacy

VO2max Lab is **not directed to children under 13**. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 because we do not collect data from anyone, but the app's content (cardiovascular fitness testing, predicted V̇O2max, training zones) is aimed at adults exercising under their own responsibility. If you are under 13, please ask a parent or guardian before using the app and consult a physician before any maximal-effort test.

## 8. Data retention and deletion

Because all data is local:

- **Per-record deletion:** delete a workout from the in-app history screen.
- **Per-profile deletion:** delete a profile from the in-app profile screen; this cascades to that profile's workouts.
- **Full app deletion:** uninstall the app from Android Settings → Apps → VO2max Lab → Uninstall. This wipes the Room database and all DataStore preferences.
- **Health Connect data:** any data we wrote to Health Connect (V̇O2max readings) stays in Health Connect after uninstall, because Health Connect is a separate app. Manage it from the Health Connect app directly.

We have no servers, no backups, no logs, no support tickets containing your data. There is nothing to forward an erasure request to.

## 10. Security

The data lives in your phone's app-private storage, which Android isolates from other apps. We rely on the device's full-disk encryption (default on Android 10+) for at-rest protection. We do not implement an in-app passcode or biometric lock; if you share a device, consider Android's per-app lock features in your launcher or in Android 14+ App Lock.

If you discover a security issue affecting the app, email **scanufe@fescacomit.com** with `[security]` in the subject line. We aim for a first response within 5 working days.
